# Naturally Occurring Blocks

> Target version: Java Edition 1.20.4. Describes each block's appearance and key traits (hardness, tool, use, spawning).

## Stone-Type Blocks

### Stone
- Appearance: a gray-white block with fine dark gray specks, the main component of the Overworld's crust.
- Traits: hardness 1.5, requires a pickaxe; obtained by smelting cobblestone, generated in large quantities underground in the Overworld; mining with a pickaxe drops **cobblestone** (Silk Touch drops stone itself). A crafting ingredient for stone tools and stone bricks.

### Cobblestone
- Appearance: gray-brown irregular chunks in a mosaic-like texture.
- Traits: hardness 2, requires a pickaxe; smelts into stone, can be crafted into furnaces, stone tools, stone bricks (3 kinds), pistons, etc. Generates in dungeons, villages, abandoned mineshafts, spawner rooms, etc.

### Granite / Diorite / Andesite
- Appearance: granite is pink-white mottled; diorite has black-and-white speckles; andesite is gray-white granular.
- Traits: hardness 1.5, requires a pickaxe; generate in large veins underground in the Overworld; each can be polished into a variant, and crafted into corresponding slabs/stairs/walls.

### Tuff
- Appearance: gray-green rock with tiny pores.
- Traits: hardness 1.5, requires a pickaxe; generates in veins below Y=0 since 1.17+; used in 1.20 archaeology sites, can be polished, and can also be crafted into tuff bricks (1.20 experimental content became 1.21's default; tuff bricks are craftable in the default version). Not destroyed by lava.

### Calcite
- Appearance: pure white with a slightly gray tone, fine-grained rock with subtle horizontal streaks.
- Traits: hardness 0.75, requires a pickaxe; generates in large veins within dripstone caves.

### Deepslate
- Appearance: dark gray, nearly black, with a rough horizontal layered texture.
- Traits: hardness 3, requires a pickaxe; generates below Y=0, the main rock of the Overworld's deep layers; mining with a pickaxe drops **cobbled deepslate** (Silk Touch drops deepslate itself). Has polished, brick, tile, and chiseled variants.

### Cobbled Deepslate
- Appearance: dark gray, chunky texture.
- Traits: hardness 3.5, requires a pickaxe; the cobblestone equivalent of deepslate, used to craft deepslate-based building materials.

### Smooth Basalt / Basalt
- Appearance: dense, dark blackish-gray rock; smooth basalt has a finer surface.
- Traits: basalt has hardness 1.5, requires a pickaxe; generates in Nether basalt deltas and soul sand valleys; can be smelted into smooth basalt (used for smithing template duplication). Basalt is arranged in a columnar orientation (`axis` state).

### Obsidian
- Appearance: deep purple-black rock with a purplish glossy sheen.
- Traits: hardness 50, requires a diamond pickaxe or better (about 10 seconds to mine); forms when water is poured onto a lava source; used for Nether portals, enchanting tables, beacon bases, ender chests, etc.; extremely high blast resistance (1200).

### Crying Obsidian
- Appearance: obsidian's texture but with glowing purple "tear" streaks on the surface (light level 10).
- Traits: hardness 50, requires a diamond pickaxe; generates in ruined portal structures; used to craft respawn anchors; cannot be used to build Nether portals.

### End Stone
- Appearance: beige/light yellow, rough, porous rock.
- Traits: hardness 3, requires a pickaxe; the main rock of the End islands; can be smelted into end stone bricks, used in End city architecture.

## Dirt-Type Blocks

### Dirt
- Appearance: brown with mottled grains.
- Traits: hardness 0.5, requires a shovel; can be tilled into farmland with a hoe; can be overtaken by spreading grass blocks/mycelium.

### Grass Block
- Appearance: brown sides topped with green grass; the side grass color varies by biome.
- Traits: hardness 0.6, requires a shovel; forms when dirt is overtaken by spreading grass; drops itself with Silk Touch or an enchanted pickaxe; can be tilled into farmland with a hoe; can be grazed by sheep (turns to dirt) or turned into a dirt path (right-click with a shovel).

### Mycelium
- Appearance: purple, speckled top surface.
- Traits: hardness 0.6, requires a shovel; unique to mushroom islands; mushrooms can grow on it; can spread to adjacent dirt; can be tilled with a hoe.

### Podzol
- Appearance: brown top surface with a fallen-leaves texture.
- Traits: hardness 0.5, requires a shovel; generates in giant taiga forests; spruce saplings can be planted on it.

### Coarse Dirt
- Appearance: rougher, more visibly grainy brown than regular dirt.
- Traits: hardness 0.5, requires a shovel; crafted from dirt + gravel; can't be overtaken by spreading grass, and most plants can't be planted on it.

### Rooted Dirt
- Appearance: brown dirt with dark brown root textures extending from it.
- Traits: hardness 0.5, requires a shovel; generates in lush caves; hanging roots can grow from its underside.

### Mud
- Appearance: dark grayish-brown, wet dirt.
- Traits: hardness 0.5, requires a shovel; formed by pouring a water bottle on dirt; can be crafted into mud bricks (mud + wheat), generates in mangrove swamps.

### Red Sand
- Appearance: orange-red, fine sand grains.
- Traits: hardness 0.5, requires a shovel; unique to desert badlands biomes; can be smelted into red sandstone.

### Sand
- Appearance: pale yellow, fine sand grains.
- Traits: hardness 0.5, requires a shovel; affected by gravity (a falling block entity); can be smelted into glass, sandstone.

### Gravel
- Appearance: gray-brown pebbles of varying sizes.
- Traits: hardness 0.5, requires a shovel; affected by gravity; has a chance of dropping flint when mined (10%), up to 100% with Fortune.

## Clay and Sediments

### Clay
- Appearance: light gray, fine, dense block.
- Traits: hardness 0.6, requires a shovel; generates on riverbeds and in swamp bottoms; smelts into bricks, crafts into a clay block; drops a clay block with Silk Touch, otherwise 4 clay balls.

### Snow Layer / Snow Block
- Appearance: white, soft snow.
- Traits: snow layers have hardness 0.1 (1–8 layers), require a shovel; can stack up and affect entity movement; snow blocks have hardness 0.2; forms via snowfall in snowy biomes.

### Farmland
- Appearance: dark brown wet / lighter brown dry tilled soil, darker when hydrated.
- Traits: hardness 0.6, requires a shovel (though tilling requires a hoe); crops can be planted on top; is hydrated within a 4-block radius of water; reverts to dirt if trampled or jumped on by an entity (crops pop off).

## Ore Blocks (Overworld)

| Ore | Appearance | Hardness | Spawn range | Drops |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Coal Ore | Gray stone with black specks | 3 | Y=0–320 (dense above ~Y=96) | 1 coal (up to 4 with Fortune) |
| Iron Ore | Gray stone with beige specks | 3 | Y=-64–320 (peaks around Y=16 and Y=232) | Raw iron (requires stone pickaxe+) |
| Copper Ore | Gray stone with orange-green specks | 3 | Y=0–96 (peaks around Y=48) | Raw copper |
| Gold Ore | Gray stone with golden specks | 3 | Dense at Y=-64–30 | Raw gold (requires iron pickaxe+) |
| Redstone Ore | Gray stone with glowing red specks | 3 | Y=-64–15 | 4–5 redstone dust (up to 12 with Fortune) |
| Lapis Ore | Gray stone with deep blue specks | 3 | Y=-64–64 | 4–9 lapis lazuli (with Fortune) |
| Diamond Ore | Gray stone with light blue-white specks | 3 | Y=-64–16 (densest at Y=-59) | 1 diamond (up to 4 with Fortune) |
| Emerald Ore | Gray stone with emerald-green specks | 3 | Mountain biomes, Y=0–320 | 1 emerald |
| Deep variant ores | Deepslate with the corresponding ore embedded | 4.5 | Below Y=0 | Same as above (deepslate diamond ore only generates below Y=-64) |

- All Overworld ores require the corresponding pickaxe tier to drop anything (coal/copper: any pickaxe; iron: stone+; gold/diamond/redstone/lapis/emerald: iron+); deep variants have higher hardness but the same tool requirements.
- Smelting: raw iron/raw copper/raw gold become their respective ingots via a furnace.
- Redstone ore emits light (level 9) and particles when mined or touched, and stops signaling once touched.

## Nether Blocks

### Netherrack
- Appearance: deep reddish-brown, rough, porous rock.
- Traits: hardness 0.4, requires a pickaxe; the Nether's main terrain block; **fire on it never goes out**; can be used for infinite lava setups (bucket scooping), etc.

### Nether Quartz Ore
- Appearance: netherrack with embedded white quartz crystals.
- Traits: hardness 0.4, requires a pickaxe; drops nether quartz; the Nether's only "ore."

### Nether Gold Ore
- Appearance: netherrack with embedded golden specks.
- Traits: hardness 0.4, requires a pickaxe; drops 2–6 gold nuggets (with Fortune).

### Ancient Debris
- Appearance: dark gray block with a brownish "debris" texture and glowing orange flecks.
- Traits: hardness 30, requires a diamond pickaxe (blast-proof); generates in the Nether at Y=8–22; smelts into netherite scrap, the only source of netherite.

### Soul Sand / Soul Soil
- Appearance: dark brown / dark, sand-like texture.
- Traits: hardness 0.5, requires a shovel; soul sand slows entities that walk into it, and is used for wither spawning platforms and creating bubble columns above lava; both surfaces can be lit with soul fire (blue), which doesn't spread.

### Blackstone
- Appearance: near-black rock with light-colored specks.
- Traits: hardness 1.5, requires a pickaxe; generates in Nether basalt deltas and bastion remnants; the Nether's "cobblestone" equivalent, craftable into stone tools, furnaces, polished blackstone, and blackstone bricks.

### Nether Bricks and Red Nether Bricks
- Appearance: dark red brick masonry texture.
- Traits: hardness 2, requires a pickaxe; crafted from nether brick items, used in Nether fortress architecture; has fence, stairs, wall, etc. variants.

### Crimson/Warped Nylium
- Appearance: crimson nylium is reddish-brown with glowing fungal specks; warped nylium is blue-green with glowing fungal specks.
- Traits: hardness 0.4, requires a pickaxe; can spread onto adjacent netherrack; corresponding fungi, roots, and fungus trees can grow on it.

### Crimson/Warped Stem, Hyphae
- Appearance: stems have a dark red/blue-green striped exterior with a pale yellow-white core.
- Traits: hardness 2, requires an axe; hyphae have hardness 0.2; stems can be stripped like logs; hyphae are the equivalent of wood (bark-covered logs). Usable as fireproof building material (non-flammable).

## End Blocks

### End Stone Bricks
- Appearance: beige, regular brick pattern.
- Traits: hardness 3, requires a pickaxe; the main building material for End cities and End ships.

### Chorus Plant / Chorus Flower
- Appearance: light purple, vertically striped stalk-like plant; the flower has light purple, curling petals.
- Traits: hardness 0.2; generates on the End's outer islands; chorus fruit is edible and randomly teleports the eater; breaking a chorus plant breaks the connected structure and drops chorus fruit; can grow on end stone.

### Purpur Block
- Appearance: light purple with dark purple streaks.
- Traits: hardness 1.5, requires a pickaxe; crafted from purpur smelted from chorus fruit, used in End cities; has pillar, slab, and stairs variants.

## Copper Family (1.20.4 default content)

### Copper Block
- Appearance: orange-red metal block, which oxidizes over time in stages: exposed copper (orange-brown mottled) → weathered copper (green-brown mottled) → oxidized copper (teal green).
- Traits: hardness 3, requires stone pickaxe+; oxidizes over time after placement (driven by random ticks, see `tick.md`); oxidation only affects appearance and crafting use, not conductivity; crafted from 9 copper ingots.

### Cut Copper and Cut Copper Stairs/Slabs
- Appearance: copper block cut into a smooth metal surface, also has 4 oxidation stages.
- Traits: hardness 3, requires stone pickaxe+; cut from copper blocks using a stonecutter; the only copper building material set in 1.20.4 (stairs and slabs each have 8 oxidation+waxed variants).

### Waxing and Wax Removal
- Right-clicking an unwaxed copper block with honeycomb applies wax, preventing further oxidation; right-clicking with an axe removes wax (returns it to unwaxed).
- Right-clicking an already-oxidized block with an axe "scrapes" off one oxidation stage (reverts it one level).
- 1.20.4 does not include copper lamps, copper grates, copper doors, copper trapdoors, or chiseled copper by default — these are 1.21 experimental content (toggleable in 1.20.3+ experiments) and unavailable in the default world.

### Lightning Rod
- Appearance: a slender, vertical copper rod (crafted from 4 copper ingots).
- Traits: hardness 3, requires stone pickaxe+; attracts lightning strikes within a 128-block radius to itself (since 1.19.3+, redirecting a player-triggered strike takes priority); emits a redstone signal for 8 game ticks when struck; usable for decoration and lightning protection.

## Miscellaneous Natural Blocks

### Ice / Packed Ice / Blue Ice
- Appearance: translucent ice sheet / opaque white ice block / deep blue, crystalline ice block.
- Traits: ice has hardness 0.5 (requires Silk Touch to keep, otherwise it disappears); packed ice has hardness 0.5 and blocks light (crafted into blue ice); blue ice has hardness 2.5 and requires a pickaxe; blue ice/packed ice don't melt from light sources, while regular ice melts into water (when not mined with Silk Touch).

### Powder Snow
- Appearance: pure white, fluffy snow layer.
- Traits: hardness 0.1; entities (except those wearing leather boots) sink into it and gradually take freezing damage; can be collected into a powder snow bucket; rabbits and foxes can hop across its surface.

### Moss Block
- Appearance: vivid green, velvety moss.
- Traits: hardness 0.1, fastest with a hoe; generates in lush caves; bone meal can spread it onto adjacent stone-type blocks to form more moss.

### Moss Carpet
- Appearance: a thin green layer of moss.
- Traits: hardness 0.1; decorative, can be placed and moved like a carpet.

### Glow Lichen
- Appearance: dark green, glowing, thread-like lichen attached to a block's surface.
- Traits: hardness 0.2, requires shears (drops itself); light level 7; can attach to most full block surfaces (on multiple faces); used for decoration and cave lighting.

### Amethyst Block and Amethyst Cluster
- Appearance: amethyst blocks are purple, translucent crystal blocks; amethyst clusters are pointed, clustered crystals that grow from budding amethyst.
- Traits: amethyst block hardness 1.5; cluster hardness 1.5, requires iron pickaxe+; budding amethyst grows clusters through 4 growth stages; breaking a cluster drops amethyst shards (affected by Fortune).
