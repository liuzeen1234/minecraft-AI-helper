# Building and Decorative Blocks

> Target version: Java Edition 1.20.4. Covers the appearance of general-purpose building materials and decorative blocks and their traits.

## Masonry Materials

### Bricks
- Appearance: reddish-brown brick masonry pattern (standard brick).
- Traits: hardness 2, requires a pickaxe; crafted from 4 brick items; has brick slabs, brick stairs, and brick walls.

### Stone Bricks and Variants
- Appearance: gray-white, regular brick pattern; cracked stone bricks have crack lines; mossy stone bricks have green moss patches; chiseled stone bricks have a diamond pattern.
- Traits: hardness 1.5, requires a pickaxe; crafted from smelted stone; used in strongholds and ruin architecture; includes walls, slabs, and stairs.

### Deepslate Bricks / Deepslate Tiles
- Appearance: dark gray brick/tile pattern.
- Traits: hardness 3.5, requires a pickaxe; crafted from smelted cobbled deepslate; has walls, slabs, and stairs.

### Polished Andesite/Granite/Diorite
- Appearance: a fine, polished surface of the corresponding stone.
- Traits: hardness 1.5, requires a pickaxe; each has slab, stairs, and wall variants.

### Basalt / Polished Basalt
- Appearance: dark gray, columnar / smooth surface.
- Traits: hardness 1.5, requires a pickaxe.

### Nether Bricks, Red Nether Bricks
- Appearance: dark red brick pattern; red nether bricks are a brighter red brick pattern.
- Traits: hardness 2, requires a pickaxe; a Nether fortress building material; has fence, stairs, slab, wall, and chiseled (chiseled nether bricks) variants.

### Blackstone Bricks, Polished Blackstone
- Appearance: near-black brick pattern / polished surface.
- Traits: hardness 1.5, requires a pickaxe; a bastion remnant building material; has walls, stairs, and slabs.

### End Stone Bricks
- Appearance: beige brick pattern.
- Traits: hardness 3, requires a pickaxe; an End city building material.

### Prismarine Family
- Appearance: prismarine is teal with dark green speckles; prismarine bricks have a regular brick pattern; dark prismarine has a deep blue-green diamond pattern.
- Traits: hardness 1.5, requires a pickaxe; an ocean monument building material; crafted from prismarine shards/prismarine crystals; has slabs, stairs, and walls; sea lanterns are listed separately.

### Purpur Block (see the Naturally Occurring Blocks doc)
- Traits: hardness 1.5; has pillar, slab, and stairs variants.

## Terracotta and Concrete

### Terracotta and Dyed Terracotta
- Appearance: undyed terracotta is a reddish-brown earthy color; the 16 dyed variants have a matte colored surface.
- Traits: hardness 1.25, requires a pickaxe; non-flammable, generally blast-resistant; smelted from a clay block, then dyeable into colored terracotta.

### Glazed Terracotta
- Appearance: 16 colors with a regular glazed pattern, and has a fixed facing direction.
- Traits: hardness 1.4, requires a pickaxe; smelted from dyed terracotta; can't be waterlogged; used for decorative flooring.

### Concrete Powder and Concrete
- Appearance: powder is a matte colored powder; concrete is a dense, saturated-color solid in 16 colors.
- Traits: powder has hardness 0.5 (affected by gravity, solidifies into concrete when it touches water); concrete has hardness 1.8, requires a pickaxe; concrete is a highly blast-resistant, clean-looking building material.

## Wool and Carpet

- Appearance: 16-color plush blocks / thin mats.
- Traits: wool has hardness 0.8, fastest with shears (breakable with any tool); **wool is a redstone insulator** (not a redstone conductor); dyeable (white wool + dye); carpet has hardness 0.1, can be laid on top of a block, and can be pushed aside; carpet is flammable.

## Glass Family

- **Glass**: a translucent, borderless pane, hardness 0.3; disappears when broken without Silk Touch; **transmits light but not redstone signals**; dyeable into 16 colors of stained glass.
- **Glass Pane**: a thin glass sheet, can be connected together; also has 16 dyed variants.
- **Tinted Glass**: black, opaque glass that blocks light but visually shows a silhouette; used to block light without blocking signals.
- **Glowstone**: a pale yellow, glowing crystal cluster, light level 15; hardness 0.3; obtained in the Nether; can be crafted into a redstone lamp; opaque but transmits light.

## Light Sources

- **Torch**: a thin stick with a yellow flame tip, light level 14; placed on the side/top of a block; can be crafted into a soul torch (blue, light level 10).
- **Lantern**: a yellow, glowing body inside a metal frame, light level 15; can be hung/placed; the soul lantern variant is blue (light level 10).
- **Campfire**: a log pile with flames, light level 15 (while lit); can cook food and block fall damage; see the Functional Blocks doc.
- **Jack o'Lantern**: an orange, glowing pumpkin face, light level 15; doesn't need an ignition source.
- **Sea Lantern**: a bright cyan-white glowing block, light level 15; used in ocean monuments; non-flammable.
- **Redstone Lamp**: dark and unlit normally, light level 15 when active; see the Redstone Mechanism Blocks doc.
- **Beacon**: a glowing pillar of light, light level 15; see the Functional Blocks doc.
- **Shroomlight**: a dark yellow, glowing block, light level 15; generates inside Nether fungus trees; non-flammable.
- **Mossy stone lamps/copper lamps**: copper lamps are 1.21 experimental content (not available by default in 1.20.4).

## Banners and Decoration

- **Banner**: a fabric banner, dyeable (16+ colors), can have complex patterns applied via a loom + banner patterns; can be hung on walls; can be combined with a shield for a matching pattern.
- **Packed Ice/Blue Ice**: see the Naturally Occurring Blocks doc (decoration and high-speed transport).
- **Carpet**: see above.
- **Painting**: an entity-based decoration (see the Mobs docs); **Item Frame/Glow Item Frame**: entity-based decorations (see the Mobs docs).

## Other Common Building Materials

- **Bookshelf**: a wooden-plank frame with blue book spines, hardness 1.5, fastest with an axe; boosts an enchanting table (15 bookshelves within a 2-block radius raise the level cap to 30); since 1.20, can store a book and quill.
- **Hay Bale**: a golden bundle of straw, hardness 0.5, fastest with a hoe; feeds horses/llamas/rabbits, and cushions fall damage (landing on it).
- **Clay Block**: a light gray, dense block, hardness 0.6, fastest with a shovel.
- **Mossy Cobblestone / Mossy Stone Bricks**: a stone surface with green moss patches; used for aged-looking architecture and spawner rooms.
- **Cobblestone Wall/Stone Brick Wall/Brick Wall**, etc.: 1.5 blocks tall, connectable, can be waterlogged.

## Common Traits of Stairs/Slabs/Walls

- **Slab**: half-height, can be stacked top and bottom (waterloggable); like walls and stairs, it's a non-full block (not a redstone conductor, can be waterlogged).
- **Stairs**: step-shaped, can turn corners; waterloggable.
- **Wall**: a 1.5-block-tall post-and-rail shape, connects to adjacent walls/fences; waterloggable; blocks line of sight.

## Wither Rose and Special Decorations

- **Wither Rose**: a black flower; spawns where a mob is killed by the wither; contact deals Wither-effect damage; flammable.
