# Crop and Plant Blocks

> Target version: Java Edition 1.20.4. Covers the appearance and growth mechanics of crops, flowers, grass, saplings, fungi, vines, and other plant blocks.

## Crops (Planting and Harvesting)

### Wheat
- Appearance: from a green sprout at stage 0 to golden wheat ears at stage 7.
- Traits: planted on hydrated farmland; drops 1 wheat + 0–3 seeds when mature (increased by Fortune); can be sped up with bone meal.

### Carrots
- Appearance: green leafy sprout (stages 0–3), orange root visible when mature.
- Traits: planted on farmland; drops 1–4 carrots when mature; eaten by rabbits (rabbits will hop over fences to eat carrots).

### Potatoes
- Appearance: green leafy sprout, the soil bulges when mature.
- Traits: planted on farmland; drops 1–4 potatoes when mature; has a small chance (2%) of also dropping a poisonous potato.

### Beetroots
- Appearance: red root + green leaves (stages 0–3).
- Traits: planted on farmland; drops 1 beetroot + 1–3 seeds when mature.

### Melon and Pumpkin
- Appearance: melons are large green striped fruits; pumpkins are orange, blocky fruits.
- Traits: the seedling stem is planted on farmland, and once mature it grows a fruit on an adjacent **dirt/farmland** block (a melon stem produces one melon per fruiting cycle); breaking a melon drops 3–7 melon slices; pumpkins can be carved (with shears) into a carved pumpkin; pumpkins can be used to build snow golems/iron golems. In 1.20.4, pumpkin/melon fruits form automatically once the stem matures.

### Sugar Cane
- Appearance: a green, bamboo-jointed stalk (up to 3 blocks tall).
- Traits: can only be planted on sand/dirt/red sand adjacent to a water source or a water-containing block; grows up to 3 blocks tall; breaking the block below drops the ones above; used for paper and sugar.

### Cactus
- Appearance: a green, spiky column (up to 3 blocks tall).
- Traits: planted on sand/red sand; entities adjacent to a cactus (except above/below) take 1 damage; breaking the block below drops the ones above; used for green dye and in emerald trades.

### Bamboo
- Appearance: a green cane with joints and leaves; bamboo shoots are earth-toned, pointed sprouts.
- Traits: can be planted on any plantable block; grows quickly (up to about 12–16 blocks); used for scaffolding, bamboo blocks, and sticks; a food source for pandas.

### Cocoa
- Appearance: brown pods hanging on the side of a jungle log (3 stages).
- Traits: planted on the side of a jungle log/stripped jungle log; drops 2–3 cocoa beans when mature; used for cookies and brown dye.

### Kelp
- Appearance: a long, dark green seaweed stalk.
- Traits: forms/grows underwater (up to about 25 blocks tall); can be smelted into dried kelp; dried kelp blocks can be used as fuel.

### Sweet Berry Bush
- Appearance: a green shrub that bears red berries when mature (stages 0–3).
- Traits: planted on any plantable block; harvest sweet berries (affected by Fortune); players take 1 damage when walking through it (unless sneaking/jumping); a food source for foxes.

### Glow Berries
- Appearance: orange, glowing berries hanging from cave vines (light level 14).
- Traits: planted on the underside of a block (cave vines); can climb and grow; harvest glow berries.

### Nether Wart
- Appearance: a red, umbrella-shaped fungal growth (stages 0–3), planted on soul sand.
- Traits: only grows on soul sand; drops 2–4 nether wart when mature; a core ingredient for brewing awkward potions; generates in Nether fortresses.

## Saplings and Fungi

### Saplings
- Appearance: small saplings matching their tree type (6 wood types + mangrove propagule + cherry sapling + azalea bush).
- Traits: planted on dirt/grass blocks, etc., and grow into trees over random ticks; can be sped up with bone meal; large tree saplings need a 2×2 arrangement; dark oak and jungle giant trees require 2×2. Mangrove propagules can be planted on mud.

### Mushroom (Red/Brown)
- Appearance: red mushrooms have a red cap with white spots; brown mushrooms have a brown cap.
- Traits: spawn in dark areas (light level <13); can grow and spread on dirt/grass blocks, etc. (via random ticks); giant mushrooms are grown with bone meal (stem + cap); an ingredient in mushroom stew. Mushrooms can't be planted under normal light levels (they'll be uprooted).

### Fungi (Crimson/Warped)
- Appearance: crimson fungus is a reddish-brown, umbrella-shaped fungus; warped fungus is blue-green and umbrella-shaped.
- Traits: generate in the Nether; planted on nylium/stems, etc.; bone meal can grow them into giant fungus trees (stem + cap + shroomlight).

### Azalea and Flowering Azalea
- Appearance: a green, low shrub / a shrub with pink-and-white flowers.
- Traits: generates in forests above lush caves; can be planted; bone meal grows it into an azalea tree (oak trunk + flowering azalea leaves).

## Grass and Flowers

### Grass and Tall Grass
- Appearance: short green tufts / two-block-tall green grass.
- Traits: grass drops itself with shears/Silk Touch; tall grass requires shears; color varies by biome; bone meal can generate more grass and flowers on a grass block.

### Fern and Large Fern
- Appearance: green fern fronds; generates in taiga and jungle biomes.

### Flowers - All Varieties in 1.20.4
- Appearance and sources:
  - Dandelion (yellow), poppy (red), allium (light purple), azure bluet (pale blue), oxeye daisy (see below), cornflower (blue), lily of the valley (white) — generate on grassy terrain in plains/forests, etc.
  - Tulip (red/orange/white/pink, 4 varieties) — plains.
  - Oxeye Daisy (white with yellow center), sunflower (yellow, faces the sun), lilac (purple), rose bush (red), peony (pink) — two-block-tall flowers.
  - Wither rose (black, created when the wither kills something).
- Traits: flowers can be planted on grass blocks/dirt, etc.; bone meal can duplicate them (two-block-tall flowers can't be duplicated this way); used for dye; pollinated by bees; small flowers can be planted in flower pots.

### Chorus Flower and Chorus Plant (see the Naturally Occurring Blocks doc)

### Hanging Roots and Rooted Dirt
- Appearance: dark brown root tendrils hanging from the underside of a block.
- Traits: decorative; generates in lush caves.

### Vines
- Appearance: green, clinging vine leaves.
- Traits: can attach to and grow along the sides and undersides of blocks; players and mobs can climb them (like a ladder); spreads randomly; harvested with shears.

### Weeping Vines and Twisting Vines
- Appearance: reddish-purple, downward-hanging vines from Nether crimson forests / blue-green, upward-spiraling vines from warped forests.
- Traits: weeping vines grow downward (light level 10); twisting vines grow upward; both can be harvested.

### Moss, Moss Carpet, Glow Lichen (see the Naturally Occurring Blocks doc)

### Lily Pad
- Appearance: a green, round leaf floating on water.
- Traits: placed on a water surface; entities can stand on it; breaks apart when a boat collides with it.

### Seagrass and Sea Pickle
- Appearance: green underwater grass blades; sea pickles are small, yellow-green glowing clusters (light level 6–15 underwater, depending on count).
- Traits: seagrass forms underwater (harvested with shears); sea pickles generate on coral reefs, can stack up to 4, and glow underwater.

### Coral
- Appearance: 6 coral types (tube, brain, bubble, fire, horn, and fan-shaped), each in vivid colors (red/blue/pink/yellow/purple).
- Traits: coral blocks, coral, and coral fans all require **being adjacent to a water source/water-containing block** to survive; they turn gray and die when removed from water; harvested with shears (while waterlogged); used for decoration and to craft prismarine gravel derivatives.

## Planting Rules Summary

- Farmland crops (wheat/carrots/potatoes/beetroots/melon-pumpkin family): must be planted on farmland; hydration speeds up growth; **uproots** back into a seed if light level is insufficient.
- Directly-planted types (sugar cane/cactus/bamboo/nether wart, etc.): have special base requirements (sand/adjacent to a water source, soul sand, nylium, etc.).
- Random ticks are the main driver of plant growth (see `mechanics/tick.md`); bone meal can force growth (subject to a cooldown).
- Most plant blocks can be broken instantly; some require shears (grass, vines, seagrass) to drop the item itself.
